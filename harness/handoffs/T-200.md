## Fixture generation notes

- `image/jpeg` writes no EXIF, so the APP1 segment (marker FFE1, `Exif\0\0`,
  little-endian TIFF: IFD0 with Orientation 0x0112 + GPSInfo pointer 0x8825,
  GPS IFD at offset 38 with one GPSLatitudeRef="N" entry) is hand-assembled
  and spliced directly after SOI. The generator re-parses its own output
  (orientation + GPS present, DecodeConfig unaffected: 600×800) and fails
  loudly on any mismatch — a run is its own audit.
- The pixel bomb is signature + IHDR (50000×50000, CRC-correct via
  hash/crc32) + IEND = 45 bytes; `png.DecodeConfig` sees 2.5 GP.
- heic.bin: size(16) + `ftyp` at offset 4 + brand `heic` at offset 8 + zero
  minor version.
- Determinism: coordinate-derived gradients, fixed quality 90; two
  consecutive runs produced identical sha256 for all 7 files (see commands).
